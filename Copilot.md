🧭 Roadmap of Mathematics for ML/AI
Core Foundations (must master first)
Arithmetic: Basic operations, exponents, logarithms, growth rates. Needed for scaling and complexity analysis.

Algebra: Equations, inequalities, polynomials, matrices. Crucial for linear models and transformations.

Set theory: Functions, relations, mappings. Underpins probability and logic in ML.

Probability: Random variables, distributions, Bayes theorem. The backbone of ML algorithms.

Statistics: Hypothesis testing, regression, variance. Core for data analysis and model evaluation.

Calculus: Differentiation, integration, multivariable calculus. Needed for optimization (gradient descent).

Linear Algebra (you didn’t list it, but it’s the most critical): Vectors, matrices, eigenvalues, singular value decomposition. Essential for neural networks, embeddings, PCA.

Supportive Topics (important but secondary)
Combinatorics: Counting, permutations, combinations. Useful in probability and discrete models.

Mathematical Analysis: Limits, sequences, convergence. Helps in understanding optimization rigor.

Applied Mathematics: Differential equations, numerical methods. Useful for advanced ML models.

Number theory: Rarely used directly, but helpful in cryptography and hashing.

Advanced/Optional (for research-level ML/AI)
Topology: Spaces, continuity, manifolds. Important in advanced deep learning (manifold learning, geometric deep learning).

Optimization theory (extra topic): Convexity, Lagrange multipliers. Crucial for training ML models.

Information theory (extra topic): Entropy, KL divergence, mutual information. Widely used in ML, NLP, and deep learning.

Graph theory (extra topic): Networks, connectivity. Needed for Graph Neural Networks.

📚 Teaching Plan
We’ll go step by step, starting from the core foundations and gradually moving to advanced topics. Each topic will include:

Concept explanation (clear and intuitive).

Examples (both mathematical and ML-related).

Practice problems (to strengthen your understanding).

Connections to ML/AI (so you see why it matters).
